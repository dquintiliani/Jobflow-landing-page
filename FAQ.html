Who is
this for?

Understanding who we built this for — and why.

We designed Jobflow specifically to help people who've recently lost their jobs — or who can see the writing on the wall and want to get ahead of it.

We believe that losing your job, or needing to make a significant career change, is one of the most stressful experiences a person can face. It's not just a financial challenge — it affects your sense of identity, your routine, and your confidence. And navigating that transition alone, without a clear guide, makes an already tough situation harder than it needs to be.

Our mission is simple: be the guide you wish you'd had. We want to help you through this bumpy time and make sure your move to your next opportunity feels as seamless as possible.

Absolutely. Many of our users are proactively planning — either because they sense instability at their company, or because they've decided it's time for a change on their own terms.

Using Jobflow before you're in a crisis means you can make decisions with more clarity and less panic. You'll know your financial runway ahead of time, understand your market value, and have a plan ready to execute the moment you need it.

Right now, we're focused on tech professionals — the salary benchmarks, job market data, and role-specific guidance is calibrated for the tech industry. This means the advice and estimates you get will be more accurate and relevant if you're coming from a tech background.

That said, the core tools — severance calculations, action plans, and financial runway estimates — can provide useful guidance for anyone with standard employment terms.

How do you
make money?

We believe you should know exactly how we sustain ourselves. No hidden agenda.

Yes — completely free. Jobflow, your personalized action plan, severance estimates, and job search timeline are all available at no cost.

If we ever introduce paid features, we'll be upfront and transparent about it well in advance. You'll never wake up to find a feature you relied on locked behind a paywall without warning.

The short version: If anything changes, you'll be the first to know — and the core tools will remain free.

We cover our costs primarily through referrals. Here's how that works in practice:

  • There are many professionals — career coaches, financial advisors, employment lawyers — who can help you navigate a layoff, but who charge for their services.
  • When Jobflow connects you with one of these professionals, and you choose to work with them, they compensate us for that referral through a pre-arranged agreement.
  • This means our incentive is aligned with yours: we only earn when we connect you with someone genuinely useful. We're not paid to show you ads or sell your data.
Important: You are never obligated to work with anyone we suggest. Referrals are always optional, clearly labeled, and only offered when we think they'd genuinely help your situation.
