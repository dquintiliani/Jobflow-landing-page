Questions

Common questions

When will the Layoff Calculator be ready?

We're currently in development and plan to begin beta testing soon. Sign up for the waitlist and you'll be among the first to get access.

Is it free to use?

The core calculator and action plan will be free. We plan to offer additional premium features for deeper guidance and job search tools.

What information do I need to use it?

Just the basics — your role, salary, years at your company, state, and layoff date. No sensitive account info or Social Security number required.

Is my data private and secure?

Completely. We never sell your data to third parties, and everything you enter is used only to generate your personal plan.

I wasn't laid off yet — is this still useful for me?

Absolutely. Many people use it to plan ahead, understand what a hypothetical severance package would look like, or prep for a voluntary job change.

Does this work for people outside tech?

Right now we're focused on tech professionals, but the calculator can provide useful estimates for anyone with a standard employment arrangement.
